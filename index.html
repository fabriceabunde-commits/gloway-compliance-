Frequently asked questions

Understand what registration means.

Is FDA food facility registration the same as FDA approval?

No. Food facility registration is not FDA approval, certification, or an endorsement of a facility or its products.

Does the FDA charge a food facility registration fee?

The FDA does not charge a fee to register a food facility. Gloway may charge separately for assistance, coordination and related services.

Do all food businesses need to register?

No. Applicability depends on the facility's activities and exemptions. We assess the circumstances before recommending a process.

How often must food facility registrations be renewed?

Registrations subject to the requirement must generally be renewed every even-numbered year between October 1 and December 31.

Can Gloway guarantee registration or import clearance?

No. FDA processes and import admissibility depend on applicable requirements and official decisions. Registration alone does not guarantee that a shipment will be admitted.
